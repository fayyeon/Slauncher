# Slauncher

A simple & lightweight launcher with one-hand reachability principles for Android. Free, open-source, and no nonsenses. Requires Android 8 (API Level 26).

For my personal-usage only, that's why I'm trying to make it as simple as possible. If you want to add your own additional features, you can fork it.

---

## Features

- App lists that started at the middle part of your screen, easier to reach with one-hand.
- Search bar that's positioned on the bottom of your screen, easier to reach with one-hand.
- Show your wallpaper or keep it simple by using solid color based on your theme (or just pure black/white), it's your choice!
